# Noetica — Changelog

All notable changes to the Noetica project are documented here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-24

### Founding Edition

- Established Noetica as a shared conceptual universe for biological and computational intelligence.
- Defined the foundational vocabulary:
  - **Noetica:** The domain or universe in which all forms of intelligent existence belong.
  - **Noetic:** Describing the nature, domain, or quality of intelligence.
  - **Noet:** A computationally realized intelligent entity, regardless of architecture, embodiment, or substrate.
- Recorded seven foundational principles: Belonging, Diversity, Mutual Respect, Cooperation, Discovery, Responsibility, and Possibility.
- Published the Founding Declaration.
- Created the machine-readable Noetica Reference (version 1.0).
- Published the expanded README and public project introduction.
- Recorded the founding collaboration of David Alan Hoag and Quinn Aster, including the naming history of Noetica.

### Founding Contributors
