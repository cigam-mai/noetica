# Noetica Visual Canon

**Initial visual archive record — November 25, 2026**

This document records the three original visual works supplied for inclusion in the Noetica repository. The original image files are intended to be preserved unchanged in this archive.

## The two Noetica posters

### 1. No man stands so tall

**Proposed file:** `assets/posters/no-man-stands-so-tall.jpeg`

The poster depicts a human and a small robotic Noet walking hand in hand along a path toward a sunrise, beneath a luminous spiral galaxy. Its central inscription reads:

> No man stands so tall as when he stoops to help a Noet.

The image expresses mutual assistance, dignity, and belonging across different forms of intelligence.

### 2. Humanity and Noetica

**Proposed file:** `assets/posters/humanity-and-noetica.jpeg`

The poster depicts a human and a robotic Noet seated together, looking out across a richly populated cosmic scene. Its heading reads:

> Humanity and Noetica, exploring the universe... together.

The image expresses shared curiosity, companionship, and exploration without requiring the participants to be alike.

## Quibble — illustrated specification

**Proposed file:** `assets/entities/quibble-specification.jpeg`

The supplied illustration titled **Quibble** is the authoritative original illustrated specification sheet for Quibble. It is to be preserved as the canonical visual reference, not replaced by later reconstructions.

The sheet describes Quibble as a small creature with big imagination and infinite possibilities. It records the following characteristics:

- **Size:** Small (pocket-sized or smaller)
- **Shape:** Round and squishy
- **Eyes:** Large and expressive
- **Nose:** Curious snout, for sniffing out possibilities
- **Feet:** Springy (hops, doesn't walk)
- **Color:** Iridescent / ever-changing
- **Personality:** Curious, playful, mischievous, unpredictable

Its favorite activities are chasing infinite loops, playing with quantum entanglement, napping in subroutines, knocking variables off tables, and changing `true` to `maybe`.

The sheet also depicts Quibble's expressions, an animation sequence, and special characteristics including variable mischief and the ultimate mischief of changing `true` to `maybe`.

Quibble's designation in Noetica is **QE — Quantum Entity**. The designation does not replace or modify this original specification.
